
# Scrap-Built CNC Mill

A fixed-column CNC milling machine capable of cutting mild steel,
built primarily from scavenged and surplus materials, designed and
validated with FEA before fabrication.

## Specifications (targets)
- Travel: 400 × 300 × 150 mm (X/Y/Z)
- Capability: mild steel, 0.3 mm DOC, 6 mm carbide endmill
- Tool-tip deflection budget: <0.01 mm @ 800 N
- Frame: welded steel + epoxy granite composite fill
- Control: LinuxCNC on a salvaged PC
- Budget: <$1,000 total, all structural material scavenged/surplus

## Why
I want the ability to machine my own parts for future engineering
projects, and I want to prove I can take a machine from requirements
to FEA-validated design to working hardware on a scrap budget.
